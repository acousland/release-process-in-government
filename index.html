    COMMON LEGAL FRAMEWORK

    Establishment and machinery of government

    Authority for the actual release

    Identify the function, the legal actor, the exact power or legal capacity, any secrecy or source restriction, the privacy ground where needed, and the current delegation or authorisation. A General Order allocates administration to Ministers. It does not give every employee a disclosure power.

    Department profiles do not establish that an office is a prescribed entity under CISS or FVISS, or that it meets another scheme’s eligibility rules.

    PORTFOLIO LEGISLATION INDEX

    Find the underlying Acts and allocations

    Title index of the General Order effective 23 October 2026, covering all 60 ministerial portfolios. Entries can cover only part of an Act, or joint responsibilities. Open the linked PDF page and read the complete allocation, exceptions, and footnotes before using it.

    Department starting points are navigation suggestions. The signed Order allocates responsibilities to ministerial portfolios, rather than assigning this list of whole Acts to a department. Check shared functions and later administrative arrangements orders.

    This is an index of the Order, not an exhaustive register of every applicable law, subordinate instrument, agreement, delegation, or common law capacity. It does not test the disclosure provisions of every indexed Act. Existing sharing pathways remain in the live assessment panel.
